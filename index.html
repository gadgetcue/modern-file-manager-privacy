Privacy Policy

Modern File Manager for TV (package com.sid.filemanager). Last updated: 2 November 2026.

This app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and it does not use the network to send any information about you or your files.

Files and storage

The app asks for "All files access" so it can browse, search, copy, move, rename, delete, compress and open files on your device and connected USB or SD storage. Files are read or changed on your device only when you ask the app to. Nothing is uploaded.

Installed apps and APKs

The APK extractor reads the list of apps installed on your device to let you save an app's APK file to your storage. This stays on the device. If you open an APK file, the system installer handles the installation and asks for your confirmation.

Settings

Your theme, favorites and view preferences are saved locally on the device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
